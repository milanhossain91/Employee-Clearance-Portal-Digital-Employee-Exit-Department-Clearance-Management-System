# Overview

Employee Clearance Portal is an enterprise-grade Employee Exit Management and Digital Clearance Management System designed to automate the complete resignation and employee offboarding workflow.

The system eliminates paper-based clearance forms and provides a centralized workflow where HR, Finance, MIS, Supply Chain, Distribution, Marketing, Credit, Training, Corporate Admin, and other departments can digitally approve or reject employee clearances.

Designed for medium and large organizations, manufacturing companies, FMCG, pharmaceuticals, corporate offices, and multi-business enterprises.
